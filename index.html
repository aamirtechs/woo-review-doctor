Woo Review Doctor

A zero-dependency Python CLI that audits a WooCommerce product reviews CSV export for the problems that quietly cost a store sales: negative reviews with no owner reply, negative reviews left unanswered past the reply window, fake-review patterns (one author leaving many reviews, identical review text, a low rating with no detail), reviews stuck awaiting moderation past the reply window, products with too few reviews to earn trust, and bad data such as duplicate review ids, malformed rows, invalid ratings, and invalid or future dates. It also prints the rating distribution per product, so a store owner can see at a glance which products are loved and which are in trouble.
